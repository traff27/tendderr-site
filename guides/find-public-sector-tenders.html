Where to find public-sector tenders in England, Wales and Northern Ireland

Last checked against official sources: 30 October 2026. General information, not legal advice.

Short answer

Start with Find a Tender, the government’s free central digital platform. Below-threshold notices must now be published there too. Then add the portals your buyers use: Sell2Wales for Wales, eTendersNI for Northern Ireland, regional portals such as YORtender for many Yorkshire councils, and eTenders for the Republic of Ireland. You shouldn’t have to pay to view public-sector opportunities.

Tenders in Leeds and Yorkshire

Many Yorkshire and Humber public bodies advertise through YORtender, including Wakefield Council and the West Yorkshire Combined Authority. Bigger contracts and below-threshold notices also appear on Find a Tender. It’s worth registering on both and choosing the categories that match your trade. Sources: Wakefield Council; West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Northern Ireland

eTendersNI is free. Register, choose the CPV categories that match your work, and the Administrator on your account receives email alerts. The Department of Finance lists a helpdesk on 0800 240 4545. Source: Department of Finance NI.

Republic of Ireland (Irish eTenders)

Irish public-sector opportunities are advertised on eTenders. If payments to you from a public-sector contract will exceed €10,000 (including VAT) in a year, you need an Irish Tax Clearance Certificate. Construction contracts also need a Relevant Contracts Tax rate of 0% or 20%. Source: Revenue (Ireland).

Quick answers

Where are UK public-sector tenders published?

On Find a Tender, the government’s free central digital platform. Below-threshold notices must be published there too. Many buyers also use regional or sector portals, such as Sell2Wales, eTendersNI and YORtender.

Are tender portals free?

Find a Tender is free, and the government says you shouldn’t pay to view public-sector opportunities. eTendersNI is also free to register.

Where do Yorkshire councils advertise tenders?

Many use YORtender, the shared Yorkshire and Humber e-tendering portal, as well as Find a Tender.

Do I need tax clearance to bid for Irish public contracts?

You need an Irish Tax Clearance Certificate if payments from a public-sector contract will exceed €10,000 including VAT in a year, according to Revenue.
