How to register on the Central Digital Platform and get your unique identifier

Last checked against official sources: 30 October 2026. General information, not legal advice.

Short answer

Registration is free and done on Find a Tender in three steps: create a GOV.UK One Login, complete your Central Digital Platform account, then register your organisation as a supplier. You’ll get a unique identifier to share with buyers. You only need to register when you want to bid. Since 1 April 2026, if you win a notifiable below-threshold contract, the buyer must have your identifier.

The three steps

  1. Create a GOV.UK One Login with your own work email address. You’ll get security codes by text message or an authenticator app. If you already have a One Login, sign in and skip to step 2.
  2. Complete your Central Digital Platform account. Agree to the privacy policy and enter your name.
  3. Register your organisation as a supplier. Enter your Companies House number or an equivalent such as a Charity Commission number. If you don’t have one, the platform gives you a unique identifier. Add your SME status, a shared organisation email address and your registered address. Then save and select ‘View’ to see your identifier.

One person sets up the account and becomes the Administrator. They can add and approve other users, and they enter and amend the organisation’s details. Source: GOV.UK guide to registering and finding your unique identifier.

Basic registration for smaller contracts

For notifiable below-threshold contracts, you only need a basic registration to generate an identifier. GOV.UK describes it as a short five-minute registration. It asks for:

  • organisation name
  • organisation address (a non-UK address is allowed)
  • email address
  • Companies House or other registry number. If you don’t have one, a Public Procurement Organisation Number (PPON) is generated for you
  • SME or VCSE status

From 1 April 2026, buyers must confirm you’re registered and get your identifier when they award a notifiable below-threshold contract. That’s £12,000 including VAT or more for central government and NHS bodies, and £30,000 or more for sub-central buyers. The same identifier is then used for all future procurements. Source: GOV.UK: new legislative requirements.

What to have ready for bigger tenders

For tenders covered by the full rules, the government lists the information you’ll need. In summary:

  • your Companies House number, if you have one
  • postal address, website and email
  • the last two years of accounts, if you have them
  • details of any people or businesses connected to your business, or with significant control over it
  • details of any convictions or offences by the organisation or connected people

Source: Cabinet Office short guide for suppliers.

Registered on the old Find a Tender?

You need to re-register. If you use the same email address, your saved searches from the old account carry over. Source: Cabinet Office short guide for suppliers.

Quick answers

Is Central Digital Platform registration free?

Yes. Find a Tender, the central digital platform, is free for everyone.

How long does registration take?

GOV.UK describes the basic registration needed for below-threshold contracts as a short five-minute registration. Full supplier information for bigger tenders takes longer.

What is a PPON?

A Public Procurement Organisation Number. It’s the unique identifier the platform generates if you don’t have a Companies House or other registry number. Once registered, you use the same identifier for all future procurements.

Do sole traders need a Companies House number to register?

No. If you don’t have a Companies House or equivalent number, the platform assigns you a unique identifier.

When do I need to register?

Only when you want to bid. From 1 April 2026, you also need an identifier to give the buyer if you’re awarded a notifiable below-threshold contract.
