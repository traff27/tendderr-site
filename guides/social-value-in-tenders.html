Social value in public tenders: what buyers score and what’s changing

Last checked against official sources: 30 October 2026. General information, not legal advice.

Short answer

Central government buyers must give social value a minimum 10% weighting of the total score in above-threshold procurements under PPN 002. From 1 January 2027, PPN 026 applies to central government procurements of £1 million or more: a minimum 10% weighting from £1m to £5m, and 20% for £5m and over, focused on good jobs and skills. Other public bodies, such as councils, can choose to follow the same approach. Whatever you commit to becomes part of the contract.

Now: PPN 002

  • Applies to central government departments, their executive agencies and non-departmental public bodies, for above-threshold procurements under the Procurement Act 2023.
  • A minimum 10% weighting of the total score for social value. Where price-per-quality-point or value-for-money methods are used, it’s 10% of the quality score.
  • Commitments made in your tender must go into the contract as terms or key performance indicators.
  • Local jobs created to deliver the contract must be advertised through the government’s ‘Find a Job’ website.
  • Buyers must avoid placing unnecessary burdens on suppliers, and must have regard to the barriers SMEs and VCSEs face.
  • Other public bodies may choose to apply it.

Source: PPN 002.

Separately, the Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 requires public bodies to consider social value when buying services.

From 1 January 2027: PPN 026 and the new Social Value Model

  • Applies to central government covered procurements of £1 million or more (including VAT) that start on or after 1 January 2027.
  • Minimum weighting: 10% for contracts from £1m to £5m, and 20% for £5m and over.
  • Two outcomes, Good Jobs and Skills, with six award criteria.
  • At least one social value key performance indicator on contracts of £5m or more.
  • Poor social value performance can be taken into account when considering whether to exclude a supplier.
  • Further guidance was due in autumn 2026, and other authorities may adopt the model.

Source: PPN 026: the Social Value Model.

Writing a social value answer you can deliver

  • Answer the question the buyer asked. Use their award criteria and words.
  • Commit only to what you can actually deliver. It becomes a contract term or KPI.
  • Give each commitment a number, a named owner and a reporting date.
  • Keep it proportionate to the size of the contract and your business.

Quick answers

How much is social value worth in a public tender?

For central government above-threshold procurements under PPN 002, at least 10% of the total score. Under PPN 026, from 1 January 2027 for central government procurements of £1m or more, at least 10% from £1m to £5m and 20% for £5m and over. Councils and other buyers set their own weightings unless they choose to follow these notes.

Do councils have to use PPN 002?

PPN 002 applies to central government departments, executive agencies and non-departmental public bodies. Other public bodies may choose to apply it. The Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 requires public bodies to consider social value for services contracts.

What happens if we don’t deliver our social value commitments?

Commitments become contract terms or KPIs, so they’re monitored like the rest of the contract. Under PPN 026, poor social value performance can be taken into account when considering exclusion.

What does PPN 026 change?

From 1 January 2027 it brings in a new Social Value Model for central government procurements of £1m or more. It focuses on Good Jobs and Skills, with minimum weightings of 10% or 20% depending on contract value.
