Security and CCTV tenders: common questions

Where are security and CCTV tenders advertised?

On Find a Tender, the free central platform, and on Contracts Finder for older notices in England. Welsh public bodies also use Sell2Wales, and Northern Ireland bodies use eTendersNI. A lot of security work is bought through frameworks, dynamic markets and wider facilities management contracts. Our free tender alert covers all of these.

Do buyers ask for SIA approved contractor status?

Often. The ACS is voluntary, but the Security Industry Authority says approval is a requirement for many public contracts. Your staff will also need the right SIA licences for the work. Check the tender documents for exactly what each buyer asks for.

Can Tendderr provide SIA licences or NSI certification?

No. Licences, ACS approval and NSI or SSAIB certification are held by your firm and your staff, and come from the SIA or the certification body. Tendderr doesn’t hold or provide them. We help you find suitable tenders and prepare your bid.

How much does Tendderr charge?

Tender alerts and the suitability check are free. Bid packs are from £495 (Essential), £750 (Standard) or £995 (Complete); framework applications from £795; DPS or dynamic market applications from £395. No commission and no VAT added. We send a fixed quote within 2 working days of receiving your tender documents.

Do you cover Scotland?

No. We cover England, Wales and Northern Ireland.
