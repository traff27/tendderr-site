IT support and telecoms tenders: common questions

Where are IT support and telecoms tenders advertised?

On Find a Tender, the free central platform, and on Contracts Finder for older notices in England. Welsh public bodies also use Sell2Wales, and Northern Ireland bodies use eTendersNI. A lot of IT and telecoms work is bought through frameworks and dynamic markets. Our free tender alert covers all of these.

Do we need Cyber Essentials to bid?

Often. Under PPN 014, central government bodies and NHS bodies must ask for Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus or equivalent controls on certain contracts, and other public bodies may do the same. Check the tender documents for what each buyer asks for.

Can Tendderr provide Cyber Essentials certification?

No. Certification is held by your firm and comes from a Cyber Essentials certification body. Tendderr doesn’t hold, provide or assess it. We help you find suitable tenders and prepare your bid.

How much does Tendderr charge?

Tender alerts and the suitability check are free. Bid packs are from £495 (Essential), £750 (Standard) or £995 (Complete); framework applications from £795; DPS or dynamic market applications from £395. No commission and no VAT added. We send a fixed quote within 2 working days of receiving your tender documents.

Do you cover Scotland?

No. We cover England, Wales and Northern Ireland.
