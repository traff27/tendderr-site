Commercial cleaning tenders: common questions

Where are commercial cleaning tenders advertised?

Public bodies such as councils, schools and academy trusts, nhs trusts, universities and colleges publish notices on Find a Tender, the government’s free central digital platform. Many also use regional portals, such as Sell2Wales in Wales, eTendersNI in Northern Ireland and YORtender for many Yorkshire councils. A lot of repeat work is bought through frameworks and dynamic markets. Our free tender alert covers all of these. See where to find public-sector tenders.

Can a small commercial cleaning business win public-sector contracts?

Yes, the rules are designed to help. For regulated below-threshold contracts, buyers can’t use a separate pre-qualification stage to shortlist suppliers, because GOV.UK guidance recognises it can be a barrier for SMEs. Buyers may also reserve some below-threshold competitions for SMEs or local suppliers. If you’re not ready to bid direct, subcontracting to a main contractor is another route in.

Is social value scored in these tenders?

Often. Central government buyers must give social value at least a 10% weighting in above-threshold procurements under PPN 002. Councils and other buyers may choose to apply the same approach. See our social value guide.

Do I need to register before I bid?

You register free on the Central Digital Platform (Find a Tender) when you’re ready to bid. From 1 April 2026, buyers awarding notifiable below-threshold contracts must get your unique identifier. See our registration guide.
