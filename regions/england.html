Contracts Finder holds older notices and contracts. Contracts Finder

Many buyers also use regional portals. In Yorkshire and the Humber, many public bodies use YORtender, including Wakefield Council and the West Yorkshire Combined Authority. Sources: Wakefield Council; West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Rules worth knowing

Since 24 February 2025, most public-sector buying in England has followed the Procurement Act 2023. Source: GOV.UK short guide for suppliers.

Since 1 April 2026, a supplier awarded a notifiable contract (worth £30,000 or more including VAT for councils and most other public bodies, or £12,000 or more for central government, including NHS bodies) must register on the Central Digital Platform and give the buyer its unique identifier. Registration is free. Source: New legislative requirements, GOV.UK.

Tenders in England: common questions

Do I have to pay to see tenders in England?

No. Find a Tender is free for everyone, with free search and alerts. Our tender alert is free too.

Where do Yorkshire councils advertise?

Many Yorkshire and Humber public bodies use YORtender, as well as Find a Tender. See where to find tenders.

Do I need to register before I bid?

You can register free on the Central Digital Platform when you’re ready to bid. Since 1 April 2026, you must be registered, and give the buyer your unique identifier, if you’re awarded a notifiable contract.
