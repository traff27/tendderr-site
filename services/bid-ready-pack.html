New rule

Won a public contract? You may now need to be registered.

Since 1 April 2026, a supplier awarded a notifiable contract (worth £30,000 or more including VAT for councils and most other public bodies, or £12,000 or more for central government, including NHS bodies) must register on the government’s Central Digital Platform and give the buyer its unique identifier. Registration is free.

Our bid-ready pack includes doing this with you on a call, signed in under your own GOV.UK One Login.

Sources: New legislative requirements under the Procurement Act 2023, GOV.UK; How to register and find your unique identifier, GOV.UK. Wales already had a similar rule under its own regulations.

What’s in the pack

We do this with you on a call. You sign in with your own GOV.UK One Login and we guide you through each step; we never log in for you. At the end you’ll have your unique identifier ready to give to buyers.

A clear summary of what your firm does, where and for whom, that you can attach to bids and send to buyers.

Questions about the bid-ready pack

Is Central Digital Platform registration free?

Yes. The platform is free for everyone. Our £195 covers doing it with you on a call, plus six core policies and a one-page capability statement. The fee is credited against a later bid pack.

Do you log in to my account?

No. You sign in to your own GOV.UK One Login and account yourself. We guide you through it on the call.

I don’t have a Companies House number. Can I still register?

Yes. If you don’t have a registry number, the platform generates a unique identifier for you.
