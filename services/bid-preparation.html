Our services

Bid preparation (bid pack)

If a tender is worth going for, we’ll prepare your submission with you for a fixed fee agreed before any work starts.

Quoted per tender, fixed fee

Fixed fee, no commission, no win fee. Prices have no VAT added: Tendderr isn’t VAT registered.

How it works

  1. We agree the scope and fee

    Every tender is different, so we look at what’s involved and quote a fixed fee before any work starts.

  2. We gather what we need from you

    Your experience, how you’d deliver the contract, and the documents the buyer asks for.

  3. We prepare the bid pack

    Answers written to what the buyer has actually asked for, in plain English, organised so it’s easy to check and submit.

  4. You check it and submit it

    It goes in under your name, from your own account on the buyer’s portal.

Honest about how we work

Our bids are AI-assisted, then reviewed and prepared by a person before anything goes to you. Calls to 0333 339 7931 are answered by a real person.

No one can honestly guarantee a win; buyers make the decision. What we can do is help you put forward a clear, complete bid.

Questions about bid preparation

How much does a bid pack cost?

Every tender is different, so we quote a fixed fee for each one before any work starts. No commission and no win fee.

Who submits the tender?

You do, from your own account on the buyer’s portal. We prepare the submission with you.

What happens if we don’t win?

The buyer must send you an assessment summary explaining how your tender was scored. Our lost-bid review is free for our own bid clients.
