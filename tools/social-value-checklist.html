About this tool

Social value is how your firm will benefit the area the contract serves: for example local jobs, apprenticeships, work with schools or charities, or cutting emissions. The Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 requires public bodies to consider social value when buying services. Read more in our social value guide.

Questions about this tool

Does PPN 002 apply to council tenders?

PPN 002 applies to central government above-threshold procurements. Councils and other public bodies may choose to apply the same approach, so check the tender documents.

What changes on 1 January 2027?

PPN 026 sets minimum social value weightings for central government covered procurements of £1 million or more: 10% from £1 million to under £5 million, and 20% for £5 million or more, focused on Good Jobs and Skills outcomes.
