Tender readiness checker

Answer eleven quick questions to see what you already have in place and what to sort before you bid for public-sector work.

Free. This tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us or stored, and there’s no sign-up.

1. Are you registered on the Central Digital Platform (Find a Tender), with your unique identifier to hand?
2. Have you completed your core supplier information on the platform, so you can share it with a buyer?
3. Are you registered on the portals your buyers use, such as Sell2Wales, eTendersNI or YORtender, with alerts set up?
4. Do you have the written policies buyers commonly ask for, such as health and safety, equality and environmental policies?
5. Do you know your insurance cover levels, and have current certificates to hand (for example employer’s and public liability)?
6. Are your latest accounts, or other financial information, ready to share?
7. Can you describe two or three recent, similar jobs (public or private), with a contact who could vouch for your work?
8. Do you hold the accreditations or registrations your trade usually needs, where relevant (for example Gas Safe, NICEIC or a health and safety scheme)?
9. Could you staff and deliver a contract of this size without hurting your existing work?
10. Could you commit to realistic social value actions, such as local jobs, apprenticeships or community work, and report on them?
11. Do you have enough time before the deadline to plan, write, check and submit?

Your next steps

  1. Register free on Find a Tender. See our registration guide, or we can do it with you on a call in the bid-ready pack.
  2. Complete your core supplier information on the Central Digital Platform and keep it up to date. Buyers ask you to share it.
  3. Register on the portals for your area and pick the categories that match your trade. See where to find tenders, or ask for our free tender alert.
  4. Write policies that reflect how your firm actually works. Our bid-ready pack includes six core policies for you to adopt.
  5. Check what the tender documents ask for: buyers often set minimum cover levels. Gather your current certificates.
  6. Have your latest accounts ready. Buyers may check your financial standing as part of the conditions of participation.
  7. Write short summaries of recent, similar jobs: what you did, for whom, its size and how it went. Ask your contacts first.
  8. Check which accreditations the tender asks for. If you don’t hold them yet, consider a smaller lot or our subcontracting route in the meantime.
  9. Be honest about capacity. A smaller lot, a partner, or subcontracting to a main contractor may suit you better for now.
  10. Use our social value checklist, or ask about a social value plan.
  11. Work back from the deadline with our tender timeline calculator. If time is tight, say so early.

This checklist doesn’t predict whether you’d win: buyers decide that, on the tender in front of them. It shows what to sort before you bid.

Questions about this tool

Does this tell me whether I’ll win?

No. Buyers decide who wins, based on the tender in front of them. This checklist only shows what to sort before you bid.

Is anything I enter saved or sent to you?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us or stored, and there’s no sign-up.

What if I’m not ready yet?

That’s common. Start with the basics: platform registration, policies and certificates. Our bid-ready pack (£195) covers registration, six core policies and a capability statement, and the fee comes off a later bid pack.
